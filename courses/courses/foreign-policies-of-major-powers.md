# Foreign Policies of Major Powers

## Course Overview

This undergraduate course examines the foreign-policy strategies of the United States,  Union, Russia, China, with attention to their historical development, strategic interests, diplomatic practices, and global consequences.

## Core Themes

- Foreign-policy theories.
- National interest and power.
- Strategic competition.
- United States foreign policy.
- European Union external relations.
- Russian foreign policy.
- Chinese foreign policy.
- Indian foreign policy.
- Major-power rivalry.
- Global and regional implications.

## Teaching Methods

- Comparative case studies.
- Student-led geopolitical discussions.
- Primary and secondary-source analysis.
- Foreign-policy briefings.
- Research projects.
- Structured classroom debate.

## Learning Outcomes

Students should be able to:

1. Explain major approaches to foreign-policy analysis.
2. Compare the foreign policies of major powers.
3. Analyze the relationship between domestic politics and external behavior.
4. Assess the impact of major-power competition on global order.
5. Prepare evidence-based foreign-policy analysis.

## Assessment and Student Engagement

Students may complete research projects, analytical presentations, written assignments, policy briefs, and continuous internal assessments.
